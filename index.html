2026 – now

Document intelligence platform for family offices

Architect and lead · concept to beta

Problem. Family offices process financial documents dense with client names, account numbers, and personal detail. Nothing could be sent to a model as-is, and the numbers inside still had to be pulled out by hand.

Solution. A platform that strips identifying information out, then extracts the figures that matter into structured data. Idea to working beta with a core team of three, scaling to nine for the final push.

React and Vite, Python redaction service, Azure OpenAI on a private endpoint, PostgreSQL, offset-based pseudonymization backed by a local entity recognition model, audit logging.

2026 – now

Labor standards from decades of work orders

Developer · heavy equipment industry

Problem. A dealer's technicians repair equipment thousands of times a year. How long each repair should take was buried in decades of unstructured work orders, so estimates were guesswork.

Solution. A system that reads that history and produces a defensible labor standard per job, with citations back to the source records and deterministic validation, reviewed by experts in a purpose-built app before it reaches the client's systems.

Snowflake, Go, citation-backed generation pipeline, deterministic validation, XML export into the client's existing workflow, evaluation harness.
