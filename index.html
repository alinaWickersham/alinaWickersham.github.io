Selected work

2026 – now

Document intelligence platform for family offices

Architect and lead · concept to beta

Family offices handle financial documents full of client names, account numbers, and personal details. This tool strips that information out, then pulls the numbers that matter into structured data. I took it from an idea to a working product, now in beta, with a small core team. I owned the architecture, wrote much of the code, ran the delivery, and worked directly with the client.

React and Vite, Python redaction service, Azure OpenAI on a private endpoint, PostgreSQL, offset-based pseudonymization backed by a local entity recognition model, audit logging.

2026 – now

Labor standards generation from historical work orders

Developer · heavy equipment industry

A dealer's technicians repair equipment thousands of times a year. How long should each repair take? The answer was buried in decades of work orders. This system reads that history and produces a labor standard for each job, with citations back to the records it came from, so a person can check the reasoning. Experts review the output in a purpose-built app before it goes into the client's systems.

Snowflake, Go, citation-backed generation pipeline, deterministic validation, XML export, evaluation harness.

2024 – 2025

AI drafting platform for legal demand letters

Architect and lead

Attorneys spend hours drafting demand letters that follow the same structure every time. We built a tool that drafts them. It cut the time roughly in half.

I was on this project from the beginning, including the stretch where it ran over budget and behind schedule. The real problem was not the technology. It was expectations that had never been set clearly with the client. Getting that right taught me more about running an engagement than anything else I have worked on. I changed the technical approach, put regular structured communication back in place, and steadied the delivery.

2025 – 2026

Conversational assistant over enterprise sales data

Solution Lead and developer · national wholesale distributor

A sales team could already query their data, but it meant setting a stack of filters and then waiting for the results to load. Now they ask a question in plain English and get an answer back. Each person sees only the accounts they are allowed to see. With a small team, I built the chatbot, the permissions model, and the knowledge base behind it.

Python, SQL plus retrieval-augmented generation, role-based access control, ERP integration, Azure.

2025 – now

Real-time dispatch optimization

Workstream owner · dispatcher dashboard

A dispatcher matches the right technician to the right job. Some of those calls are for right now, and some are planning a week out. The information needed for both lived in separate systems that did not talk to each other. Our team brought it together into two screens: one for making and changing assignments in real time, one for making smart assignments when planning ahead. I owned the dashboard work and contributed to the routing, integration, and write-back services underneath it.

Redis pub/sub and streams, WebSockets, React, route-optimization APIs, PostgreSQL.
